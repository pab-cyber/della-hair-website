About Della Hair

12+ YearsColour & cutting experience

Meet Della

Colour is the craft. Trust is the point.

Della Hair started with a simple frustration: too many colour appointments feel rushed. So the studio was built the opposite way — private, unhurried, one client in the chair at a time.

Every appointment opens with a proper consultation: your hair history, your lifestyle, how much upkeep you actually want to commit to. From there, colour is mixed and hand-painted specifically for you, not pulled from a fixed formula.

Based in Walton-on-Thames, the studio has built a loyal client base across Surrey — from Weybridge to Hersham — through word of mouth and, increasingly, through a growing following on Instagram.
